THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

Can I Do That? is designed to work without an account and without sending your skill progress or stories to us.

What stays on your device

The site can remember things such as your skill statuses, custom skills, hidden skills and stories.

This information is stored locally in your browser on your device.

It is not sent to Can I Do That? or stored on our servers.

Your stories

If you add a story to a skill, the information you write stays in your browser on your device.

Can I Do That? does not receive or read your saved stories.

Backups

You can create a backup of your saved Can I Do That? information.

The backup is downloaded as a file to your device. Can I Do That? does not receive a copy.

You can use that file later to restore your saved information.

Clearing your data

Because your information is stored in your browser, clearing browser data or site data may remove your saved skills and stories.

If you want to keep them, create a backup before clearing your browser data or moving to another device.

Cookies and analytics

Can I Do That? does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers.

We do not currently use cookies to store your skill progress or stories. The site uses local browser storage instead.

Accounts and advertising

Can I Do That? does not currently have user accounts, subscriptions or advertising.

Changes to this page

If the way Can I Do That? handles information changes, this page will be updated to explain what has changed.

Questions

A contact address will be added here when a public Can I Do That? contact method is available.
