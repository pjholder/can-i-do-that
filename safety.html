THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

Safety

Last updated: September 2026

Can I Do That? is about trying things, learning things and experiencing more of the real world.

The real world also comes with risks.

Use your judgement, know your limits and take the time to do things properly.

Start where you are

People have different abilities, experience and circumstances.

Something that is easy for one person may be difficult or unsafe for another.

Before trying something, think about whether it is appropriate for you, your surroundings and your level of experience.

There is nothing wrong with starting small, getting help or deciding that something isn't for you.

Learn before you leap

Some skills need more than a quick explanation.

Activities involving things such as tools, fire, heat, water, heights, roads, vehicles, electricity or other potential hazards may require proper instruction, equipment, supervision or experience.

If you don't know how to do something safely, find out before attempting it.

Where professional training or qualified instruction is appropriate, use it.

Use the right equipment

Use suitable equipment for the activity and make sure you understand how to use it properly.

Check equipment before using it and follow relevant safety instructions.

Protective equipment should be used where appropriate.

Think about where you are

Your surroundings matter.

Consider other people, traffic, weather, surfaces, space, animals and anything else around you that could affect an activity.

An activity that is reasonable in one place may be unsafe or prohibited somewhere else.

Respect other people, private property and the places you use.

Follow local rules

Laws, regulations and safety requirements differ between places.

You are responsible for checking and following the rules that apply where you are.

Some activities may require permission, supervision, training, licences or particular safety equipment.

Children and young people

Some activities on Can I Do That? may not be suitable for children or young people to attempt alone.

Parents, guardians and supervising adults should decide what is appropriate and provide suitable supervision where needed.

Age restrictions and local rules should always be followed.

Listen to yourself

Being curious doesn't mean ignoring your limits.

If something feels beyond your ability, experience or circumstances, stop.

Get help, learn more, try an easier version or come back to it another time.

You don't have to prove anything by completing a skill.

In an emergency

Can I Do That? is not an emergency or professional advice service.

If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, contact the appropriate emergency service where you are.

The point

Try things.

Be curious.

Learn from people who know more than you.

Make mistakes you can safely learn from.

Look after yourself and the people around you.

Then go see what you can do.
