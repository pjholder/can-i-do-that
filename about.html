Why this exists

Life is something to take part in.

But it’s becoming easier to spend more and more time behind a screen — watching other people make things, learn things, explore, create and live.

Can I Do That? is a small push in the other direction.

A place to find things to try, things to learn and things you might never have thought of doing.

Useful things. Useless things. Everyday things. Strange things.

MAKE LEARN MOVE EXPLORE CONNECT

Use your hands. Move your body. Make something. Learn something. Talk to someone. Go outside. Try something you might be terrible at.

You don’t need to master everything.

You don’t need to turn it into a career, a side hustle or something productive.

Sometimes doing something just because you’re curious is enough.

Being human isn’t something you watch.

It’s something you do.

And there’s probably far more you’re capable of than you know.

So, can you do that?
